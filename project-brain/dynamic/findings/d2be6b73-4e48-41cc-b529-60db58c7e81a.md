# The child-login toggle was a second, unthrottled account-creation surface

## Goal
AD-004 ruled /join/{code} the only registration surface and rate-limits it. FR-008's child-login toggle also calls CreateNewUser but carried no limit, so an authenticated guardian could mint unlimited accounts on arbitrary addresses; the uniqueness failure became the account-enumeration oracle AD-004 exists to prevent, and an address could be squatted so its owner could never register. Fixed with a limiter keyed per guardian and address, refusals audited; AD-004 amended to record the second surface.
